SocialAgent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SocialAgent is a personal tool used only by its developer to upload videos to the developer's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to third parties.

What the app accesses

The app uses OAuth to obtain permission to upload videos to, and read basic profile information about, the developer's own accounts (Google/YouTube, Instagram, TikTok).

How data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the developer's own computer, in the macOS Keychain.
  • The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties.
  • The app does not collect data about any people other than the developer.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's account settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions), and stored tokens can be deleted from the macOS Keychain.
