SocialAgent

SocialAgent is a personal tool that schedules and uploads the owner's own short chess videos to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not available to the public and does not offer any service to other users.

The app uses the TikTok Content Posting API only to upload videos to the TikTok account owned by the app's developer.
